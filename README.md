### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a developer based in Durgapur, India, holding an MCA and specialized certifications in AI/ML (Simplilearn & Anthropic Academy). I specialize in architecting highly scalable backend systems, deploying applied AI solutions, and building event-driven full-stack applications. 

Recently, my focus has shifted toward **self-hosted LLMs**, **Vector Databases**, and **Serverless Cloud Infrastructure**—moving beyond managed services to build cost-effective, high-performance AI architectures. 

* 🔭 **Currently Building:** AI-driven SaaS products (like Outloop) and real-time event platforms using **Go, FastAPI, AWS, and Vue/Next.js**.
* 🧠 **Deep Dive Into:** Self-hosted LLaMA models, Qdrant vector search, advanced RAG pipelines, and hybrid cloud deployments.
* 🚀 **Looking For:** Impactful engineering roles (AI Platform, Backend, Full-Stack) or entrepreneurial teams tackling complex architectural challenges.

### 🆕 Recent Milestones & Projects

* **Self-Hosted AI & Recommendation Engines:** Architected and migrated a production AI pipeline from GCP Vertex AI to a self-hosted **LLaMA 32B** model deployed on a VM, utilizing **Qdrant** for high-speed vector search and embeddings.
* **Real-Time Platforms (Go & AWS):** Developed and scaled concurrent event and queue management systems (*Brain Ground*, *RoundWait*) using **Go, Firebase, Vue 3,** and **AWS infrastructure**.
* **Serverless & Payments Integration:** Built robust subscription and payment pipelines using **Stripe**, **AWS Lambda**, and **Angular** for secure, low-latency transactions.
* **Startup Initiatives:** Architecting the foundational AI pipeline and platform infrastructure for *Outloop*, an outbound sales AI tool geared toward accelerator standards (like Y Combinator).
